Communication

Zones — INA route or man moving toward another zone: scream “IN IN IN.”
Zones — OUTA route or man moving out toward another zone: scream “OUT OUT OUT.”
Nobody's thereIf the teammate responsible for the next zone is gone, do not leave the route. Stay with it — nobody is there to take it.
Tiger — SWITCHCorner and outside linebacker have man. Routes cross and a teammate screams “SWITCH” — switch people.
Rock & Roll and Bracket cancel everythingEither one kills the call that came before it — front, coverage and blitz. Trips shows, we're in Rock & Roll. Bunch shows, we're in Bracket.
Cancelled means cancelledTight Tiger Mike is called, they come out in trips, we go Rock & Roll. Mike does not go. The blitz died with the call.
Motion back outThey motion out of trips and it's no longer trips — drop back to the original call. Tight Tiger is on again.

Strength & Playside

1. Trips or BunchThree receivers to a side — that's the playside. Beats everything below it.
2. TE on the lineNo trips: strength is the TE's side.
3. Opposite the backNo trips, no TE on the line: strength is away from the RB.
4. Wide sideLast resort: the side with more grass between the ball and the sideline.
Rock & Roll and Bracket split jobs by playside. Whichever side you're aligned to decides which rule you play.
